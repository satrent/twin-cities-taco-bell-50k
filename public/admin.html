Backups & new year

1. Download a backup

Saves a complete copy of the site's data: every runner from every year, the notify list, and the settings. Keep it somewhere safe, because it contains runners' personal information.

Download backup

To look through runners in a spreadsheet, use Runners > Export CSV instead. The backup file is for restoring the site if something goes wrong.

2. Start a new year

This permanently deletes every runner, including emergency contacts, waivers, bibs, and notes, from every year. Then it gets the site ready for the next race:

  • The race year changes to the year you enter below.
  • Registration switches to Coming soon.
  • The race date, deadlines, refund cutoff, and packet pickup go back to "To be announced."
  • The fee, capacity, start time, and payment instructions stay as they are.
