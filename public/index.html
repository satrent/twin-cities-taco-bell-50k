The route

Point to point across the metro, from Bloomington to Oakdale. Seven Taco Bells are your unofficial aid stations. We'll give you a Strava GPS map to follow, and you don't need a paid account to use it.

The rules

  1. Eat at all seven

    Consume a food item from the menu at every stop. Drinks don't count. Eating on the go is fine, and you can eat at the first stop before the start.

  2. One burrito. One crunchwrap.

    At least one of each, and they can't both be at the final stop. Breakfast versions count. Vomiting and assistive medications (like Pepto Bismol) are permitted.

  3. Save the evidence

    Keep a receipt from each stop, print or digital, and record your 50k with a GPS app like Strava.

  4. Plan your bathroom breaks

    Use the Taco Bell stops whenever possible. Off-course breaks are permitted as needed.

  5. Finish in 12 hours

    Cross the line within 12 hours of the start to earn your medal and glory.

Good to know

  • All runners must be 18 or older.
  • Menus vary by location and time of day. The start line has the full and breakfast menus from 5–11am, while Richfield and Edina have only a limited breakfast menu in the morning.
  • Order ahead in the Taco Bell app or send a proxy, but you need to be on site at each stop to get your item.
  • Grace will be given! This is all about fun, so just do your best.

FAQs

Is this a formal race?

No, this is a self-guided fun run at your own risk. There's no guaranteed on-course support like port-a-potties or water stations, no medical support on site, and no closed roads. You'll be running in high-traffic areas that require waiting for cars, and you'll likely be solo for most of the course.

I'm not a competitive runner. Can I participate?

All running and walking paces are welcome. Official finishers complete the 50k within 12 hours, about 2.5 miles per hour without stopping. Handcyclists and wheelchairs are welcome too.

Can I participate with a dietary restriction?

Yes! This run is vegetarian and vegan-friendly. Gluten-free and other dietary restrictions can also be accommodated. Consult us to learn more.

Is transportation available? What's parking like?

Please coordinate your own ride or use public transit. This is a point-to-point course, so if you drive to the start you'll need a way back to your vehicle. Please avoid parking in the Taco Bell lot. Nearby residential areas and a park should have spots, but no promises.

Can I use drive-thrus?

Foot traffic is generally prohibited at drive-thrus for safety reasons, so please go inside. Ordering ahead in the app is allowed. You don't need to order for yourself, but you need to be on site to retrieve your item.

Will there be prizes? Will the run be timed?

There's a small prize for top finishers in men's, women's, and nonbinary categories. There's no chip timing, and finish times won't be uploaded anywhere. Your GPS app is your record.

When is packet pickup?

Dates: To be announced. You'll pick up from the coordinator's house in St. Paul, and participants will get the address by email.

When is the registration deadline?

To be announced. Spots are limited and past years have sold out.

How will I follow the course?

The course isn't blocked off and has no signage. We'll provide a Strava GPS map, and you don't need a paid account to use it.

Are pacers allowed?

We can't control pacers on a self-guided run, so it's up to you. A pacer or run buddy can make your run safer, and they participate at their own risk.

Can I volunteer?

Yes. Contact us to learn about opportunities to support the runners.

What does the medal look like?

Some runners prefer not to see the medal before they finish, so we may not share it in advance.

What if I need to drop out?

Let us know as soon as possible so someone else can have a shot at this taco-fueled dream. Refund cutoff: To be announced.

Any tips?

Gastric training is highly recommended. Plan thoughtful hydration and extra nutrition on top of your menu items. Streets aren't closed, so stay vigilant and prioritize your safety at all times.

Is this officially associated with Taco Bell?

No. This event is not sponsored by or endorsed by Taco Bell Corp.
